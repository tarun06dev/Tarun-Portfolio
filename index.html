MACHINE LEARNING / HEALTHCARE · 1STOPAI INTERNSHIP

Predicting Mortality of Heart Failure

Built and compared two mortality-prediction models (SVM and a 4-layer neural network) on 299 patient records across 12 clinical features. Used correlation heatmaps and swarm/boxen plots to identify key risk indicators like ejection fraction, serum creatinine, and age.

The neural network (TensorFlow/Keras, with dropout and early stopping) reached 81% accuracy and raised recall on at-risk patients to 82%, versus 57% for the SVM baseline — reducing missed high-risk cases.
